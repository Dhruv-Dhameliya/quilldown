Privacy

Your writing stays on your device

Quilldown is a Markdown editor that runs entirely in your browser. What you write is never sent to a server, there's no account to create, and no cloud copy exists. This page explains what that means, what it doesn't cover, and how you can check it yourself.

At a glance

The short version

Nothing is uploaded

Typing, previewing, copying and exporting all happen on your device. Your text isn't sent anywhere.

No account

There's no sign-up, no email address and no login, so there's nothing to link your writing to.

Saved on your device only

Your drafts autosave in your browser so they're there when you return. That's the only copy.

Open source

The code is public on GitHub, so anyone can read what Quilldown does.

What's stored, and where

What Quilldown keeps, and where it lives

Quilldown keeps two kinds of information, and both stay in your browser's storage on your own device.

Your documents

Every open tab is autosaved to your browser's local storage as you type. Pasted images are stored with the document. These drafts exist only on your device. Nothing is copied to a server, so we can't see them, recover them or hand them to anyone.

Your preferences

Quilldown remembers a few settings, such as your theme (light or dark) and whether you chose "Start in editor". These are stored the same way, in your browser, on your device.

That's the full list

There's no profile, no history of what you've written and no record of which documents you've opened.

How to remove it

To delete a draft, close its tab. To remove everything Quilldown has stored, including your preferences, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Once it's deleted, it's gone, because there's no other copy.

What Quilldown doesn't do

What we don't do

Kept on your device

  • Your open tabs and drafts
  • Images you pasted into a document
  • Your theme, light or dark
  • Your "Start in editor" choice

Never happens

  • We don't upload your text, at any point, for any reason.
  • We don't ask you to make an account or give an email address.
  • We don't measure or record what you write.
  • We don't load third-party scripts, fonts or icons while you work. Everything the editor needs ships with the site.
  • We don't send your documents to any AI service, converter or export server. Every export is created in your browser.
Exporting and converting

Converting a document doesn't send it anywhere

Many online converters upload your file to their servers to process it. Quilldown doesn't. PDF, Word, PNG, EPUB, HTML, Markdown and plain text files are all generated inside your browser and saved straight to your device.

The PDF export uses your browser's own print dialog, so it works the same way: your document is turned into a file on your computer, not on ours.

Sharing

How sharing works without storing your document

When you click Share, Quilldown compresses your document and packs it into the link itself. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is saved on a server.

This works because of how web addresses are built. The part of a link after the # symbol stays in the browser and isn't sent to the website's server when someone opens it. Whoever opens your link gets their own private copy in a new tab.

Sent to the serverquilldown.vercel.app/ Stays in the browser#d=z.eJxLz3Mo…

What to know before you share

  • Anyone who has the link can read the document. There's no password or expiry, because there's no account or server to enforce one.
  • The link contains your content. If you paste it into a chat app, email or social post, that service can see the link, and so the document inside it.
  • Pasted images aren't included in shared links.
  • Long documents make long links. You'll see a warning above 8,000 characters and a hard stop at 64,000.
  • Once you've sent a link, you can't take it back.

If a document is sensitive

Don't share it as a link. Export it and send the file through a channel you trust.

Offline and third parties

Built to work without the outside world

Nothing loads from outside

Every library, font and icon Quilldown uses is bundled with the site. Nothing loads from third-party servers while you work, so there's no outside company that can see that you're using it.

Cached after your first visit

A service worker caches the site on your device. From then on, Quilldown loads and exports without a connection. In browsers that support it, you can also install it as an app.

Offline is a privacy option too

If you want to be sure, turn off your connection and keep writing. Everything still works.

Check it yourself

Don't take our word for it

You can verify all of this in a couple of minutes.

  1. Open Quilldown and press F12 (or right-click and choose Inspect) to open your browser's developer tools.
  2. Go to the Network tab.
  3. Type a few lines in the editor, switch tabs, and try an export.
  4. Watch the list. You won't see requests carrying your text.

For a stronger test, load the page once, switch off your Wi-Fi and keep writing. Everything continues to work.

You can also read the source code on GitHub and see exactly how Quilldown stores, renders and exports your writing.

What this doesn't protect against

The honest limits

"Nothing is uploaded" is a strong promise, but it isn't the same as "nothing can go wrong". These are the things worth knowing.

  • Other people using your browser. Drafts are stored in your browser profile. If someone else uses your device and your browser profile, they can open Quilldown and see them. On a shared or public computer, clear the site's data when you finish, or use a private window.
  • Browser extensions. Extensions you've installed can sometimes read the pages you visit. If you write sensitive material, check which extensions you trust.
  • Clearing browser data. Deleting your browser data deletes your drafts. Nothing is stored elsewhere, so they can't be recovered. Export important documents as .md files.
  • Shared links. As explained above, a link is your document. Treat it like the document itself.
  • Copying and pasting. When you copy text and paste it into another app, that app's privacy rules apply from then on.
  • Images linked from the web. If you add an image by its web address, your browser fetches it from that address, as it would on any web page. Images you paste or upload stay inside the document.
  • Your device and network. Quilldown can't protect a device that's been compromised. It also doesn't change what your internet provider or workplace network can see about which sites you visit.
  • The hosting of the website itself. Quilldown is served as static files by a hosting provider (Vercel). Like any website host, it receives the standard details of a page request, such as your IP address and which page you loaded, and it may keep ordinary server logs. Those requests never contain your writing.
Cookies and analytics

Cookies, trackers and analytics

Quilldown doesn't use cookies or trackers on your writing, and nothing about what you write is measured or sent anywhere. The only things it keeps are your own drafts and a few preferences, all in your browser's storage on your device.

Quilldown doesn't run analytics on its pages.

Open source

Open source, so it can be checked

Quilldown's code is public. That means developers can read how it works, security-minded people can check the claims on this page, and anyone can report a problem.

Privacy questions

Privacy questions

Can you see what I write?

No. Your text stays in your browser and is never sent to us, so we can't see it.

Do you store my documents on a server?

No. Drafts are saved in your browser's storage on your device. There's no server copy and no account.

Can you recover a document I lost?

No. Because nothing is stored on our side, there's nothing to recover. That's the trade-off for real privacy, so export important documents as .md files.

Is a shared link private?

The document isn't stored on a server, but anyone with the link can read it, and any app you paste the link into can see it. For sensitive documents, send a file instead.

Does Quilldown use cookies?

It doesn't use cookies on your writing. It keeps your drafts and a few preferences, like your theme, in your browser's storage on your device.

Does exporting to PDF or Word upload my document?

No. Every export is created in your browser and saved straight to your device.

Can I use it offline to be extra sure?

Yes. After your first visit, Quilldown works without a connection.

How do I delete everything?

Close your tabs, or clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove all drafts and preferences.
