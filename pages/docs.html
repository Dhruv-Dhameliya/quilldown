Good to know

Good to know before you start

  • You don't need Quilldown to follow along. Markdown works in any editor. The examples simply open in Quilldown so you can try them.
  • Examples open in a new tab, so your current document isn't replaced.
  • Limits worth knowing: files up to 5 MB each, up to 30 tabs open at once, and about 5 MB of browser storage in total.
  • Because nothing is stored on a server, export important documents as .md files.
